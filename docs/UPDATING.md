# How to update the Net Impact IU website

For officers. No code, no GitHub account needed. Changes go live about 2 minutes after you save.

## Getting in

1. Accept the email invite from Pages CMS (the site maintainer sends it).
2. Go to https://app.pagescms.org and sign in with the link it emails you.
3. Open **Net-Impact-IU / website**. The forms are in the left menu.

## Common tasks

| I want to... | Form | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a new officer or position | **Officers** | Click **Add**, fill in name and role, upload a square headshot, write a photo description ("Headshot of Jamie Davis"), Save |
| Remove a position | **Officers** | Open the item's menu, **Delete**, Save |
| Change the order of the board | **Officers** | Drag items up or down, Save |
| Post an event | **Events** | **Add entry**, fill in name, date, time, location; turn on **Show on home page** for big events; Save. It disappears the day after it happens |
| Add or remove gallery photos | **Photo gallery** | Add: upload the photo, caption, photo description. Remove: delete the item. Drag to reorder. Save |
| Add a member quote | **Testimonials** | Add the quote and name, then turn on **Permission to publish** only after the person says yes in writing |
| Add a client project | **Project teams** | Fill it in, and turn on **Client approved publishing** only after the client agrees |
| Post news | **News posts** | Add title, date, a one-line summary, photo and the post |
| Update stats or awards | **Impact** | Edit the numbers and labels, Save |
| Change meeting time, email or links | **Site settings** | Edit, Save |
| Change words on a page | **Page text: (page name)** | Edit, Save |

## Photo rules

- JPG or PNG, at least 800 px wide, under 2 MB. Headshots should be square.
- Always fill in **Photo description**: say what's in the photo. It's required for screen readers and helps Google.
- Only post photos of people who are OK being on the site.

## Writing rules

- Plain, confident, friendly. No all caps, no slang, no hype words.
- Say "Net Impact IU" instead of "we" in the FAQ, so answers make sense on their own in Google.
- Keep the **Google title** under 60 characters and **Google description** under 160. The form counts for you.

## If something looks wrong

- Wait 2 minutes and refresh. Most "it didn't change" moments are just the update in progress.
- Still wrong? Message the website maintainer with the page and what you changed. Every save is recorded, so nothing is ever lost.
